# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to **security@patchclock.com**. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you expect. We will acknowledge your report within 2 business days and keep you updated until it is resolved.

We will not take legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and that gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

## Supported versions

PatchClock is a hosted service. Only the version running at patchclock.com is supported.
