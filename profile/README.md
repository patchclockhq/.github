Since **11 September 2026**, manufacturers of software sold in the EU must report actively exploited vulnerabilities within 24 hours. From **11 December 2027**, every essential requirement of the CRA applies.

PatchClock helps small teams that sell installable PHP and Laravel software meet those obligations without a compliance department:

- **Incident runbook** with the 24-hour, 72-hour and 14-day clocks and draft reports ready to review
- **Composer-native SBOM** and alerts when a dependency gets a known vulnerability
- **Support period register**, disclosure policy and a security.txt that stays valid
- **Customer notice templates** and a guided EU Declaration of Conformity

Run the [free CRA readiness check](https://patchclock.com/#check) to see where you stand today.
